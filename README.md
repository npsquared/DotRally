# Dot Rally

A two-paddle ball game drawn on a recreation of an old pocket-handheld dot-matrix LCD screen, with square-wave beeps.

**Play:** https://npsquared.github.io/DotRally/

Everything is in one self-contained file, `index.html`. It has no dependencies or build step, so you can also just open it in a browser.

## Modes

- **1 vs CPU** at three levels: Easy, Medium and Hard.
- **2 players** on the same device. On a phone, each player drags on their own half of the court.

Games are first to 5, 7 or 11 points. The ball speeds up on every hit, and where it meets your paddle sets the bounce angle. Settings are saved in your browser.

## Controls

| Keyboard | Touch / mouse | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrows or WASD (1 player) | Drag on the court, or move the mouse | Move your paddle |
| W/S or A/D (player 1), arrows (player 2) | Drag on your half | Move paddles in 2-player mode |
| P or Space | Tap the pause button | Pause / resume |
| Esc | Tap MENU | Menu |
| M | Tap ♪ | Sound on/off |

Phones held upright get a tall court with paddles at the top and bottom. Wide screens get a court with paddles on the left and right.
